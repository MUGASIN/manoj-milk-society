Who We Are

A Community Built on Dairy Tradition

M.A.S Milk Society is a cooperative milk society rooted in Marudhadu village, Siriya, Tamil Nadu. We bring together local farmers and families under a shared commitment to quality, freshness, and honest dairy practices.

Our society ensures that every drop of milk collected is tested, stored properly, and delivered to you at its freshest — supporting livelihoods while nourishing families across the region.
